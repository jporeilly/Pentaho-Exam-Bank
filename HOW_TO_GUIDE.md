# Pentaho Exam Bank — How-To Guide

How to use the Pentaho Exam Bank to hold, write, review and publish the
certification exam questions for the Pentaho Content Manager's courses.

The guide is a set of pages under [`docs/`](docs/), one per task. In the app
they are under **System → Documentation**, with search, and **AI Chat** answers
questions from them.

## The usual path

1. **Courses** — [adopt a course's exam into the bank](docs/guides/02-courses.md).
2. **Bank** — [edit questions](docs/guides/05-the-bank.md), and
   [move them through review](docs/guides/06-review.md).
3. **Report** — [check each exam against the bar for its certification level](docs/guides/07-report.md).
4. **Publish** — [write the approved questions back into the course](docs/guides/09-publishing.md),
   and push them to the courses repo so learners get them.

**Generate** and **Import** add questions from other places; **AI Chat** answers
questions about the app and about Pentaho's products; **Exam paper** prints a
PDF exam; **Settings**, **Admin** and **Documentation** look after the app.
